SIGNAL TRACE // combat prototype

Steer the signal across programs to arm them, then SPACE to trace back and fire the chain.

Controls

Setup: drag a program onto an empty tile to move it, or onto another to swap — then START.
Combat: Arrows / WASD move signal  ·  Space fire chain  ·  click an enemy to target it. Layout is locked.
Each move = +1 combo (+10% signal strength). Moving decrements enemy cooldowns. Orange bars are walls.

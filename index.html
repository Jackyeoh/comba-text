Controls

Setup: drag a program onto an empty tile to move it, or onto another to swap — then START.
Combat: Arrows / WASD move signal  ·  click an enemy to target it. Layout is locked.
Move-activate: each step fires the tile you land on — "on arm" and "on commit" alike. A landed hit clears your combo.
Commit + traceback: stepping ARMS programs — "on arm" ones pay out as you pass, "on commit" ones wait. SPACE fires the armed chain in reverse; the signal then respawns and combo resets.
Each move = +1 combo (+10% signal strength). Moving decrements enemy cooldowns. Orange bars are walls.
From move 20, the enemy gains +20% power every 10 moves — stalling is not a strategy.
Block charges are listed separately with their own countdowns — two grants expire a move apart, not together.
Any damage that gets through resets your combo — blocked damage does not, so GUARD protects the streak as well as the health.
POWERS below are toggle-able run modifiers; they persist across Reset so two runs can be compared on the same picks.
